== Keep Tests That Take the Keyboard and Mouse Out of Each Other's Way

A computer has one pointer, and one window at a time has the keyboard.
Tests that open real menus, bring a window to the front, or move the
pointer fail when another project's tests take them at the same time.
DesktopInputLock holds one lock that every such test shares, across
processes and projects, and tells the person at a Mac, by banner and
aloud, when to step away and when to come back.

It is extracted from the copies in Lota, Tokra, and Deckbuilder, and
ships a +desktop_input_lock+ command for shell scripts. Source: https://github.com/setdef/DesktopInputLock
